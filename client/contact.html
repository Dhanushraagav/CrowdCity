Frequently Asked Questions

How do I report a civic issue?
Click 'Report Issue' in the navigation menu. Select your location on the interactive map, choose the appropriate category (such as Roads, Sanitation, or Streetlights), provide details, and submit to dispatch municipal authorities.
How can I track my complaint?
Open 'My Complaints' from the sidebar to review the status progression (Pending, Assigned, In Progress, Resolved), assigned field officer, and resolution notes in real-time.
How do I update my profile?
Click on your user menu in the top right header and select 'My Profile' or 'Settings' to update your personal details, language preferences, and notification channels.
What happens after submitting a complaint?
CrowdCity AI routes your report directly to the relevant municipal department. An acknowledgment tracking ID is generated, and you receive milestone updates until final verification.
