WHAT CROWDCITY DOES

Solving Civic Redressal & Public Transparency

CrowdCity AI bridges the gap between citizens and municipal authorities, replacing bureaucratic opacity with verifiable accountability.

The Civic Challenge

Traditional municipal grievance reporting has historically suffered from manual paperwork, unverified reports, delayed response times, and lack of real-time visibility for citizens into complaint resolution progress.

The AI-Powered Solution

CrowdCity AI automates issue triage, routes complaints to responsible local bodies using GPS coordinates, enforces SLA countdown deadlines, and provides citizens with a transparent 5-stage live status tracker with photo proof.

Inclusive Citizen Empowerment

By providing full bilingual access in Tamil and English, 1-tap emergency helplines, and personalized welfare scheme discovery, CrowdCity AI bridges the digital divide for every citizen across Tamil Nadu.

ENGINEERING ARCHITECTURE

Technologies Powering CrowdCity AI

Built with modern web standards, resilient cloud data stores, and real-time civic intelligence.

Modern Web & PWA

Semantic HTML5, responsive CSS3 with AMOLED Dark & Light themes, and offline-capable Progressive Web App architecture.

Node.js & Express REST

Scalable, high-throughput REST APIs handling issue triage, officer delegation, real-time SLA computation, and secure session routing.

Supabase PostgreSQL

PostgreSQL relational database with Row-Level Security (RLS), Realtime WebSocket updates, and encrypted photo evidence storage.

Groq AI & Llama 3

AI-driven automated complaint categorization, severity assessment, recommended municipal department routing, and welfare assistance.

QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything search engines, AI models, and citizens need to know about CrowdCity AI.

What is CrowdCity AI?

CrowdCity AI is an AI-powered civic technology and citizen redressal platform built for the state of Tamil Nadu. It allows citizens to report public issues such as potholes, garbage dumps, streetlights, and water leakage with live GPS coordinates and photo proof, follow resolution updates in real time, discover government welfare schemes, and access emergency helplines.

Who founded CrowdCity AI?

CrowdCity AI was founded by Sandeep Kumar J. He is an entrepreneur and visionary focused on building technology-driven solutions for smarter, more connected, and transparent public communities through AI innovation.

Who developed CrowdCity AI?

CrowdCity AI is developed by an engineering and leadership team: Dhanush Raagav S (Developer), Tulasiram V (Test Engineer), Aathisankar A (Software Architect), Padmadev D (DevOps Engineer), Aakash M (End-to-End Developer), Praneesh P (Systems Engineer), and Gladwin Levis J (API Engineer), with founder Sandeep Kumar J.

What problem does CrowdCity solve?

CrowdCity AI solves the lack of accountability and long delays in municipal grievance handling. It replaces manual paperwork with digital tracking, automated AI triage to municipal departments, GPS location validation, strict SLA response clocks, and photo-verified resolutions.

What is CrowdCity's geographic scope?

CrowdCity AI operates across the state of Tamil Nadu, India, covering all 38 districts including Chennai, Coimbatore, Madurai, Tiruchirappalli, Salem, and Tirunelveli, spanning municipal corporations, municipalities, taluks, and town panchayats.

What are the major features of CrowdCity?

Core features include: GPS-enabled civic issue reporting with photo upload; AI auto-categorization and department triage; 5-stage case progress tracking with SLA clocks; Municipal Authority inspection and dispatch console; AI welfare scheme finder; 1-tap emergency helplines; and full bilingual access in English and Tamil.

What technologies power CrowdCity AI?

CrowdCity AI is built on semantic HTML5, responsive CSS3 with AMOLED dark mode, Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+), Node.js, Express RESTful APIs, Supabase (PostgreSQL, Realtime, Storage, Auth), and Groq AI / Llama 3 for intelligent triage and conversational assistance.

What is the purpose and vision of CrowdCity AI?

The purpose and vision of CrowdCity AI is to establish transparent, citizen-first civic governance across Tamil Nadu. By equipping citizens with intelligent reporting tools and giving municipal authorities automated triage and SLA tracking, CrowdCity ensures public grievances are acknowledged and resolved promptly.

How can citizens contact CrowdCity AI?
