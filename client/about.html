PLATFORM ARCHITECTURE

How CrowdCity AI Works

From instant citizen reporting to municipal dispatch and scheme guidance, explore our end-to-end technology pipeline.

1. Multilingual AI Intake

Citizens submit grievances in English or Tamil. Our Groq AI LLM automatically parses issues, extracts GPS coordinates, and assigns severity scores.

2. Smart Municipal Dispatch

Reports are instantly routed to relevant ward officers, municipal departments, and district helplines with real-time status audit milestones.

3. Secure Vault & Schemes

Access 4-digit MPIN-protected document wallet and receive intelligent AI guidance for State and Central Government welfare schemes.
